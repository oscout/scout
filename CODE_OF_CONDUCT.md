# Code of conduct

The Scout community should be a place where people can investigate difficult
systems, disagree precisely, and improve the work without making participation
hostile.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical risk and generous about intent.
- Support claims with reproducible evidence when practical.
- Respect privacy, security embargoes, and the public/private repository
  boundary.
- Make room for different levels of experience and different communication
  styles.
- Accept a maintainer decision to narrow, defer, or close work without turning
  the disagreement into personal conflict.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Publishing another person's private information or credentials.
- Deliberately exposing an unresolved vulnerability.
- Sustained disruption, bad-faith demands, or personal attacks.
- Using project spaces to promote unrelated products, campaigns, or spam.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, and other material that
violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact routes at
[arach.dev](https://arach.dev). Do not include sensitive conduct reports in a
public issue.
